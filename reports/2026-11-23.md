- Liquidity: Market liquidity stays neutral; deficits keep adding to incomes.
- Fed: Fed near 3.88%; inflation (3.1%) keeps cuts slow.
- Markets: Returns come from income more than multiple expansion; dispersion stays high.
- Watch for: Claims stay low; Credit spreads stable; Core inflation drifts down slowly.

- Fed: Fed cuts because inflation lets it, not because growth breaks.

- Markets: Equities de-rate from a thin risk premium; Treasuries and cash outperform.
- Watch for: High-yield spread above 5.3%; Jobless claims 4-week average above 244k; Sahm indicator at 0.3 or higher.
